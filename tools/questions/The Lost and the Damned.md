# The Lost and the Damned - questions for the author

Source: `/home/claude/src/The_Lost_and_the_Damned.txt` (line numbers below).
Module: `tools/armies/the_lost_and_the_damned.py`.

The catalogue holds: the Blackshields force (standard Force Organisation Chart: generic Legiones Astartes Army List
without Praetors, plus Reaver Lord, Blackshield Marauder Squad, Chymeriae Squad, Oath of Moment and Chymeriae Attribute
configuration, Blackshield Armoury / Weapons / Xenos Weapons injected into every armoury and weapon list) and the
"Agents of the Sigillite Allied Detachment" force (Preceptor, five Knights-Errant, Strike Force Squad, Operative Cell,
Assassin). Allegiance is Loyalist or Traitor, because the book allows both for Blackshields (L214) and Agents of the
Sigillite are Loyalist only (L1569).

## Unclear / missing points

1. **Shattered Legions (L68-158).** Needs the units and rules of two or three Legions at once, which one catalogue
   cannot provide. Built: rule text only (linked from the Oath of Moment entry). Question: should Shattered Legions
   be a separate catalogue (or a gamesystem-level change allowing several Legion catalogues in one Detachment)?

2. **Delegatus Consul without Praetors (L204).** The Legion data hides the Delegatus when a Praetor is in the
   Detachment. Blackshields cannot take Praetors; built: the Delegatus is hidden when a Reaver Lord is present instead,
   and the Reaver Lord counts as Master of the Legion. Question: is the Reaver Lord the Master of the Legion, and may a
   Delegatus be taken alongside him?

3. **Sigillite Rosette (L1842 vs L2204).** Listed as standard wargear of the Preceptor and also as a 20-point
   "Preceptor only" Armoury item. Built: free standard wargear, not purchasable. Question: is it included in the 125
   points or must it be bought?

4. **Rad Grenades (L333 vs L499).** Outlanders' "Blackshield Characters may also purchase Rad Grenades" implies Rad
   Grenades are restricted, but they are listed as normal (non-Xenos) Blackshield Wargear. Built: available to every
   force. Question: are Rad Grenades restricted outside Outlanders?

5. **Xenos Deathlock category (L816 vs L844).** The table says "replaces Bolter or similar Basic Weapon", the entry
   says "Special Weapon; Xenos". Built: a Basic Weapon (replaces Bolter / Bolt Pistol slot on Characters and Veteran
   Sergeants), Xenos (Outlanders or The Alien Brotherhood only). Question: confirm.

6. **Outlanders' Xenos access (L331 vs L761).** "Items marked Xenos in the Blackshield Armoury" vs "Xenos Weapons
   specifically marked as available to Outlanders" (none is marked so). Built: Outlanders get the Xenos Wargear and the
   Xenos-marked Blackshield melee weapons and Deathlock; the conventional Xenos Weapons (Shuriken, Splinter, Fusion
   Gun, Lasblaster, Blaster, Shredder) only with The Alien Brotherhood. Question: confirm.

7. **Profiles missing.** Lascarbine, Autogun, Laslock, Heavy Chainsword (Marauders, L1320-1332) and the Blackshield
   melee weapons (L999-1081) have no printed profiles. Built: Lascarbine 18" S3 AP- Assault 2; Autogun and Laslock
   24" S3 AP- Rapid Fire; Heavy Chainsword +1 S Two-Handed; melee weapons from their text. Question: give profiles.

8. **Psyk-out Ammunition (L1972 vs L2044).** One entry gives a separate profile (24" S4 AP5 Rapid Fire, Psi-shock), the
   other says the weapon keeps its normal profile. Built: the separate profile plus the Psi-shock rule. Question: which?

9. **Officio Assassinorum Assassin (L2555-2591).** Must come from the Talons of the Emperor list, which is another
   catalogue. Built: an Elites entry with a required Temple choice at the listed cost, no profile or wargear (use the
   Talons of the Emperor entry for rules). Question: OK?

10. **Reaver Lord armoury (L1159).** Built: Space Marine Armoury capped at 75 points; Blackshield Armoury and Blackshield
    Weapons capped at 50 points together (Blackshield Weapons are chosen in the weapon slots, their cost is moved from
    the 75 to the 50 cap); Master-crafted Weapon +10 separately (not counted in either cap). Question: does the
    Master-craft count towards the 75 points?

11. **Reaver Retinue (L1205).** Built: the Reaver Lord's retinue choice offers a Legion Veteran Squad (Blackshields
    version) as "Reaver Retinue" in addition to the normal Command Squad / Terminator Command Squad. Question: OK?

12. **Rite of War.** The book does not mention Rites of War for Blackshields; the Legion Rite of War entry is still
    available. Question: may a Blackshields force use a Rite of War?

13. **Ashes of the Armoury - Desperate Warriors (L425).** Built: -2 points per non-vehicle model of every Legiones
    Astartes Army List unit (including the Blackshield units) when the Oath is chosen. The "minimum 1 point per model"
    is not checked. Question: do the Blackshield-specific units (Marauders, Chymeriae, Reaver Lord) count as "selected
    from the Legiones Astartes Army List"?

14. **Iron Halo of Agents (L2190, L2739).** Built as a separate item "Iron Halo (Agent of the Sigillite)" (4+
    invulnerable) so it does not interact with Legion Iron Halo limits. Question: OK?

15. **Cell Specialist disciplines (L2550).** Built: the discipline adds its weapon (Force Weapon / Power Weapon / Stalker
    Bolter / Master-crafted Power Weapon) to the Specialist's kit without removing his Bolter or Close Combat Weapon.
    The Champion's WS 6 and the other effects are text. Question: what does each discipline weapon replace?

16. **Macer Varren (L2850-2915)** has no "Loyalist only" line, unlike the other Knights-Errant. Built: Loyalist only
    (as all Agents of the Sigillite). Question: confirm.

17. **"Agents of the Warmaster" (L313, L409).** No such list exists in the data set. Built: text only.

- Shattered Legions rules and restrictions; The Whole is Greater than the Sum.
- Allied Detachment restrictions of the Oaths (No Gods, No Masters: no Allied Detachment with a Primarch; Shunned and
  Distrusted; Unlikely Allies / Eldar allies; Outcasts Among Outcasts: no Imperial allies). Chaplains are hidden under
  Orphans of War and The Alien Brotherhood; Agents of the Sigillite show an error under those Oaths.
- Eldritch Tutelage (Eldar psychic power), Void Reavers, The Shadow of Oblivion, Scarce Ammunition / The Last Magazine,
  Brothers Before Masters, Brother-Slayers, Inured to Pain, The Lure of Battle.
- Chymeriae Attribute effects and Genetic Instability (the Attribute is chosen as configuration, effects are text).
- Lord of the Blackshields "must be the Warlord"; Agents of the Sigillite may not be the Warlord; Assassin may not be
  the Warlord.
- Orders of the Sigillite; Agent of the Sigillite / Knight-Errant joining rules.
- "Only Blackshield Characters, Veteran Sergeants and models with Armoury access" for individual Blackshield weapons
  beyond the slots built; "no model may select the same item twice" is enforced only inside each armoury group.
- All weapon special rules (Overpressure, Deathlock, Lethal Exposure, Psi-shock, Stasis Anomaly ...).
