## Armoury

2. **Death Mask - which Independent Characters?** L124: "Any Blood Angels Independent Character may purchase a Death Mask for +10 points."
   *Built:* a "Blood Angels Wargear" option on the Legion Praetor and Legion Centurion (all Consuls). It does not count towards the 100-point Armoury cap. The named characters (Raldoron, Zephon, Crohne, Nassir Amit) cannot buy it, because their entries only list Krak grenades / Melta bombs.
   *Question:* May the named Independent Characters buy a Death Mask too? Should it count towards the Armoury cap?

3. **Inferno Pistol - where and how.** L128: "Any Blood Angels model with access to the Space Marine Armoury may purchase an Inferno Pistol for +15 points."
   *Built:* an extra item (+15) in the Armoury of the Praetor and Centurion ("Additional Wargear", counts towards the 100-point cap). It is also in every sergeant/champion "Space Marine Armoury (max 50 pts)" group, and those include the Terminator sergeants' Armoury. It is an extra weapon, not a replacement for the Bolt Pistol.
   *Question:* Should models in Terminator Armour be able to take it (pistols are normally not allowed with Terminator Armour)? Is it an extra weapon, or should it replace the Bolt Pistol?

4. **Moritat's two Inferno Pistols.** L128: "A Legion Moritat may replace both of his Bolt Pistols with two Inferno Pistols for +20 points."
   *Built:* the Moritat Consul has an option "Two Inferno Pistols (replace both Bolt Pistols)" for +20. It removes the Consul's second Bolt Pistol and empties the Centurion's "Replace Bolt Pistol" Armoury slot.
   *Question:* Is +20 the total for both pistols (as built)? Can a Moritat with this option still swap a pistol from the normal Armoury? (As built he can't.)

5. **Blade of Perdition - who and what it costs.** L142: "Any Blood Angels Character with access to the Space Marine Armoury may purchase a Blade of Perdition for +25 points. A model already equipped with a Power Weapon may exchange it for a Blade of Perdition for +10 points."
   *Built:* wherever a Character model with Armoury access can choose a Power Weapon from a weapon list, a Blade of Perdition is offered beside it. It costs +10 where the Power Weapon is free (basic wargear), +25 everywhere else. This includes sergeant lists where the Power Weapon costs +10 and the Praetor/Centurion Armoury (where the Power Weapon is 15). Characters that carry a Power Weapon as fixed kit with no weapon list (e.g. the Command Squad's Legion Champion) get no exchange. Not offered: named characters, Azkaellon, the Crimson Exemplar (no Armoury access).
   *Question:* Is +25 right in lists where the Power Weapon itself costs +10 or 15? Should fixed-kit Power Weapons (Legion Champion) also be exchangeable for +10?

6. **Over-charged Engines - which Rhinos?** L153: "Any Blood Angels Rhino may purchase Over-charged Engines for +15 points."
   *Built:* option on the Legion Rhino Armoured Carrier only.
   *Question:* Does the Damocles Command Rhino (or any other Rhino-chassis vehicle) also count as a "Rhino" here?

7. **Furioso-pattern Jump Pack.** L157.
   *Built:* +55 option on the Legion Contemptor Dreadnought, max one per army. An error appears if the Contemptor does not have two Dreadnought Close Combat Weapons. Movement and the ban on Deep Strike are text only.
   *Question:* Can a Furioso Contemptor still take its Dreadnought Drop Pod? (The book says it "may not deploy using Deep Strike", and Drop Pods arrive by Deep Strike.) As built, the Drop Pod is still offered.

## Consul

8. **Sanguinary High Priest.** L160-184.
   *Built:* Consul for the Legion Centurion (+45) with Narthecium, Reductor, Legion Support Officer (so he can't fill the compulsory HQ), Apothecarion, Sanguinius' Chosen and Furious Charge. All Centurion options stay available.
   *Text only:* "counts as an Apothecary for all rules" (L175); Furious Charge for the unit he joins (L179).
   *Question:* Since he is a Legion Support Officer, he cannot be the compulsory HQ - is that intended?

## Rites of War

9. **The Day of Revelation.** L211-238.
   *Built (enforced):* Legion Veteran Squads that took "Jump Packs (entire squad)" become Troops and count for compulsory Troops while this Rite is chosen. Max one Heavy Support choice. An error appears if the Detachment includes a Fortification. Tactical, Assault and Breacher Squads keep counting as normal Troops.
   *Text only:* the Warlord must have a Jump Pack (L234); at least half of the non-Vehicle units must be entirely Jump Pack models (L235); all Jump Infantry start in Reserve and must Deep Strike (L236); The Day is Revealed, On Wings of Fire, Angelic Shock Assault.
   *Question:* L218 says "Legion Assault Squads remain Troops choices normally." Do Tactical and Breacher Squads also still count as compulsory Troops (as built)? Or must the compulsory Troops be Assault Squads / Jump Pack Veterans only?

10. **The Day of Sorrows.** L241-267.
    *Built:* the Fortification error. The compulsory-Troops limit (Tactical, Assault or Breacher Squads, L264) is already the default, so nothing changes.
    *Text only:* The Bitter End, Sorrow Becomes Fury, No Death Unremembered, Hold Until the Last; no voluntary withdraw / must Pursue (L265-266).
    *Question:* Under this Rite, may other Troops (e.g. units made Troops by other rules) still be taken as non-compulsory Troops?

## Units

11. **Dawnbreaker Cohort.** L302-401.
    *Built:* 4 Dawnbreakers (35 each) + Champion = 210, up to 5 more Dawnbreakers. Any model (Champion included) may swap the Falling-star Spear for a Pair of Equinox Blades (free). Squad-wide Krak (+2) / Melta bombs (+5). Champion: 50-point Armoury. No Dedicated Transport (none listed).
    *Question:* Is the Champion's own Spear swap and the lack of a transport correct? The Champion's Armoury may offer a Blade of Perdition only as an extra weapon (+25), because he has no Power Weapon - fine?

12. **Crimson Paladin Squad.** L468-554.
    *Built:* 2 Paladins (45 each) + Exemplar = 160, up to 2 more Paladins. Any model (Exemplar included) may swap the Power Weapon for a Power Fist (+10) / Thunder Hammer (+15). One Paladin may swap the Coriolis Shield for a Heavy Flamer (+10), Assault Cannon (+15) or Plasma Blaster (+15). Exemplar: Grenade Harness (+10). Transports: Land Raider Phobos, Land Raider Proteus, Dreadclaw, Spartan.
    *Question:* L554 says "a Land Raider" - are both Phobos and Proteus meant? The Exemplar has no Armoury access, so he gets no Blade of Perdition - correct?

13. **Angel's Tears Squad.** L609-710.
    *Built:* 4 Erelim (30 each) + Arch-Erelim = 210, up to 5 more. Every model carries Two Volkite Serpentas (Volkite Serpenta profile). Heavy weapon pool: 1 per 5 models, at squad level. "Assault Cannon with Suspensor Web" uses the Assault Cannon profile plus the Suspensor Web rule, because no profile is printed. Arch-Erelim: Chainsword swap and 50-point Armoury. No Dedicated Transport.
    *Text only:* Destroyer Cadre - only a Moritat (or Zephon) may join (L647).
    *Question:* Is there a separate profile for the Assault Cannon with Suspensor Web (e.g. Assault 3 when moving)? Does the Erelim who takes a heavy weapon keep his second Serpenta (as built: yes)?

14. **Ofanim Court.** L755-828.
    *Built:* 2 Ofanim (50 each) + Prefector = 150, up to 2 more. Krak/Melta per model, Jump Packs +15 per model (the unit type on the profiles changes to Jump Infantry). No Dedicated Transport and no options for the Prefector, because none are listed.
    *Question:* May the Ofanim Court take a Dedicated Transport when it has no Jump Packs? Does the Ofanim Prefector get any Armoury access?

15. **Grav Chariot Squadron.** L833-883.
    *Built:* 1-3 Grav Chariots at 65 each (Fast Attack), any of them may swap the Heavy Bolter for a Multi-Melta (+15) / Assault Cannon (+20). "Twin-linked Bolters" uses the Twin-linked Bolter profile. Grav Engines is text only.

16. **Sanguinary Guard (retinue only).** L894-976.
    *Built:* 3 Sanguinary Guard for 175 (55 per model + 10 for the squad), up to 3 more for +55 each. The squad can only be chosen as a retinue of the Legion Praetor, Raldoron or Sanguinius; it is never a separate root unit. Any model (Azkaellon included) may swap the Angelus Boltgun for an Inferno Pistol (+10). Azkaellon (+35) replaces one Guard and is limited to one per army. Krak/Melta per model includes Azkaellon (L1313).
    *Text only:* the character must begin the battle joined to the squad (L935); Master of the Seraphim Deep Strike condition (L1291-1292).
    *Question:* Is the squad price right (3 models = 175, so +10 for the squad over 3 x 55)? Can Azkaellon swap his Angelus Boltgun ("Any model")?

    *Question:* Correct that Crohne is not Master of the Legion?

20. **Nassir Amit.** L1354-1404. *Built:* Master of the Legion, Power Weapon + Rending Weapon, Furious Charge, Command Squad retinue, with no allegiance restriction (none is given). The Iron Halo works like Raldoron's.
    *Question:* Is he available to both Loyalist and Traitor armies?

## Sanguinius

21. **Sanguinius.** L1497-1669.
    *Built:* Lord of War, 510, Loyalist only, Jump Infantry (Character). Free choice of Blade Encarmine or Spear of Telesto. Infernus is printed as "Assault 2, Melta, One Use"; One Use is only shown in the profile type (there is no rule entry for it). Primarch Retinue: Legion Honour Guard Squad or Sanguinary Guard. The Honour Guard may take "Jump Packs (entire squad)" for +10 per model (all or none); this changes their unit type and removes their Dedicated Transport. The normal Legion Terminator Command Squad option is not offered, because L1666-1668 lists only those two.
    *Question:* Is it right that Sanguinius cannot take a Terminator Command Squad as his retinue? Is there a rule text for "One Use"?
