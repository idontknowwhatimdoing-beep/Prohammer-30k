# XIII - Ultramarines: questions for the author

Source: `/home/claude/src/legions/XIII_Ultramarines.txt` (line numbers below). Module: `tools/legions/xiii_ultramarines.py`.

## Legion rules

1. **Flexible Command Structure - "up to four HQ selections"** (L17). Done: the normal HQ maximum (2) is raised by +2 to
   4 for the Detachment. The Master of the Legion limit is unchanged. Question: is 4 the total for the Detachment (as
   built), or 4 *extra* on top of the normal 2?
2. **Codex Astartes - Combat Squads** (L7-12). Shown as text only. The builder does not split a ten-model Tactical Squad
   or share out its Victory Points / transport. Question: OK as text only? Does a Combat Squad still count as a
   compulsory Troops choice on its own? (Built: no, the squad is one selection.)

## Armoury

3. **Legatine Axe - who may buy it** (L38 "Any Ultramarines Independent Character with access to the Space Marine
   Armoury"). Done: +20 under "Additional Wargear" in the Praetor's and Centurion's (all Consuls') 100-pt Armoury, counts
   towards the cap. Not given to named characters (they have no Armoury). Question: is it an extra weapon (as built) or
   should it replace a weapon (e.g. the Chainsword/Power Weapon)?
4. **Legatine Axe profile** (L39-44 give only Strength "User +1" and "Power Weapon, Two-Handed"; L233 does not mention
   Two-Handed). Done: `- / User +1 / - / Power Weapon, Two-Handed` for everyone (the Suzerains too). Question: is
   Two-Handed meant for the Suzerains' axes, given they also carry a Boarding Shield and a Bolt Pistol?
5. **Mantle of Ultramar cost** (L49 "An Ultramarines Praetor equipped with Artificer Armour may exchange it for a Mantle
   of Ultramar for +20 points"). Done: an option in the Praetor's Armour choice at +40 (Artificer Armour +20, then +20 for
   the exchange). Centurions/Consuls cannot take it. Question: correct total? Does the Mantle keep any invulnerable save
   (the text only gives 2+ Armour and Feel No Pain 5+)?
6. **Breacher Power Weapons** (L55-56 "Any model in an Ultramarines Legion Breacher Siege Squad may replace its Bolter
   with a Power Weapon for +5"). Done: a squad block "replace Bolter with Power Weapon (any number)" for the Breacher
   Marines, capped at one per model minus the models that took a Specialist Weapon; the Breacher Sergeant also gets
   Power Weapon (+5) in his "Replace Bolter" choice (instead of a combi-weapon). Question: is the Sergeant included in
   "any model"?

## Rites of War

7. **The Logos Lectora - additional compulsory HQ** (L127). Done: errors if the Detachment has fewer than 2 HQ choices,
   or has neither a Master of Signals Consul nor an HQ Damocles Command Rhino. A Damocles taken as the Master of Signals'
   Dedicated Transport does not count. Question: OK?
8. **The Logos Lectora - Troops and Deep Strike** (L128, L131). Done: error with fewer than 3 Compulsory-Troops-eligible
   units; error if the Detachment contains a Legion Drop Pod, Dreadnought Drop Pod or Anvillus Dreadclaw Drop Pod.
   Question: are Dreadclaws "required to enter play using Deep Strike" (built: yes)? Are Deep Striking units with Jump
   Packs/Terminators simply forbidden from *using* Deep Strike (built: text only)?
9. **The Logos Lectora - Tank/Flyer vs Infantry** (L129). Not enforced (the builder cannot compare counts of two
   unit-type groups cleanly). Shown in the rite text. OK?
10. **Vigil Opertii Mission - allied Militia Detachment** (L154-156). Not enforceable (the Militia Detachment comes from
    another catalogue). Done: errors for a Traitor army and for a missing Vigilator Consul; Reconnaissance Squads become
    Compulsory Troops eligible while the rite is chosen. Their "Support Squad" rule is still listed on the unit. OK?

## Units

11. **Invictarus Suzerain Squad 0-1** (L238). Done: 0-1 per Detachment for the Elites version; Suzerains taken as a
    retinue are not counted towards it. Question: should the 0-1 also cover retinue Suzerains?
12. **Suzerains as retinue for Ventanus and Prayto.** L237 allows them for any named Ultramarines IC "permitted to select a
    Command Squad", but Ventanus (L861) and Prayto (L1081) only list "one Legion Command Squad". Done: both may take a
    Command Squad or Suzerains. Gage (L793) and Guilliman (L1380-1383) list them explicitly. Centurions cannot (L237 names
    only the Praetor). Correct?
13. **Suzerain Legate** (L229, L273). Done: +20 (65 pts instead of a 45-pt Suzerain), 50-pt Space Marine Armoury; his Bolt
    Pistol can be swapped through the Armoury. The Armoury is the normal Sergeant list (Artificer Armour removed since he
    has it). OK?
14. **Fulmentarus Terminators - Dedicated Transport** (L426 "a Land Raider, Dreadclaw Drop Pod or Spartan Assault Tank").
    Done: Land Raider Phobos, Land Raider Proteus, Anvillus Dreadclaw, Spartan. Question: which Land Raider patterns? Can
    a Dreadclaw carry Terminators?
15. **Fulmentarus - Guided Warheads needs a Nuncio Vox** (L378) but the squad has no Nuncio Vox option. Built as listed.
    Should the squad (or the Decurion) be able to take a Nuncio Vox?
16. **Locutarus Storm Squad** (L557-571). "Up to two Locutarus" may swap Bolt Pistols (built: max 2 in the squad,
    Strike Leader not included - he swaps through his 50-pt Armoury). The Strike Leader's Armoury skips Artificer Armour.
    No 0-1 limit is printed and none was built. OK?
17. **Nemesis Destroyer Squad - Mortifier Bolter** (L671-672). Profile built as the normal Bolter (24", S4, AP5, Rapid
    Fire) plus Poisoned (3+) and Pinning. The Sergeant also carries one (unit Wargear list). The Sergeant's Chainsword
    swap, Artificer Armour and Phosphex Bombs (up to 3) are separate from his 50-pt Armoury cap. OK?
18. **Nemesis Destroyer - "Land Raider"** (L736). Done: Land Raider Phobos or Proteus (same as Breachers). Correct?
19. **Nemesis Destroyer - Missile Launcher swap** (L701-704 "For every five models in the squad, one ..."). Done: 1 per 5
    models including the Sergeant (1 with 5-9 models, 2 with 10). OK?

    Dreadnought Close Combat Weapon's built-in Twin-linked Bolter is listed as a separate weapon. The profile has no Hull
    Points / Wounds. Question: is he 0-1 / unique as built? Any upgrades (e.g. Extra Armour, Havoc Launcher like other
    Contemptors)?
