## Legion rules

1. **Daemonic Covenant Detachment not built.** L25-44: "A Traitor Word Bearers army may include a Daemons of the Ruinstorm Covenant Detachment, selected from the Daemons of the Ruinstorm Army List."
   *Built:* the full rule text (Covenant FOC, 25% cap, Daemonic Summoning, Summoning Points) is shown on the Legion entry. This data set has no Daemons of the Ruinstorm army list, so the Detachment cannot be added.
   *Question:* Should a Daemons of the Ruinstorm catalogue (and a "Covenant Detachment" force type with the 0-1/1-3/0-1/0-1/0-1 chart and the 25% check) be made? That needs a shared game-system change.

2. **The Dark Shepherds.** L12-13: "must include at least one Legion Chaplain Consul or Legion-specific Dark Apostle as an HQ selection."
   *Built:* an error on the Legion entry unless the Detachment has a Chaplain Consul, any Accursed Crozius (only Praetors/Centurions can buy one), Erebus, Kor Phaeron or Zardu Layak.
   *Question:* There is no separate "Dark Apostle" unit in the text - is a Praetor/Centurion with an Accursed Crozius the only "Legion-specific Dark Apostle"? Does Lorgar (or Hol Beloth / Argel Tal) count?

3. **Two different "From Beyond" texts.** L80-89 (a block before the Armoury: Ruinstorm Covenant may use the normal Allied chart, 25% cap removed, Sworn Brothers, Manifested/Summoned) and L182-184 (inside the Rite: an Allied Detachment from "Codex: Chaos Daemons" as Sworn Brothers).
   *Built:* both texts are combined in the Dark Brethren rule.
   *Question:* Which one is current? Is "Codex: Chaos Daemons" meant to be the Daemons of the Ruinstorm list? Can an army then have both a Chaos Daemons ally and a Covenant?

4. **Text only (not enforced):** Fanatical Devotion (L7), Ritual of Consecration incl. "no effect in a Loyalist army" (L18-21), Daemonic Summoning and Summoning Points (L47-57), Daemonology note (L61).

## Armoury and Consul

5. **Accursed Crozius - cap and slot.** L97: "Any Word Bearers Praetor or Centurion may purchase an Accursed Crozius for +40 points."
   *Built:* an extra item in the Praetor/Centurion Space Marine Armoury "Additional Wargear" (counts towards the 100-pt cap, max 1). It does not replace a weapon.
   *Question:* Does it count towards the Armoury cap? Does it replace the model's melee weapon or come in addition? Can a Chaplain (who already has a Crozius Arcanum) take it?

6. **Tainted Weapon - who.** L109: "Any Word Bearers Character eligible to select a Power Weapon may instead select a Tainted Weapon for the same points cost."
   *Built:* added beside every Power Weapon option on every Character model (Praetor, Centurion, Sergeants, Champions, Dark Martyr, Overseer, Possessed Champion, Procurator Prime, Iconoclast...). Non-character "any model" lists do not get it.
   *Question:* Are Sergeants (Characters) meant to get it, or only Independent Characters?

7. **Burning Lore.** L122: "Praetor, Centurion, Chaplain or Diabolist which is not already a Psyker".
   *Built:* +30 option on Praetor and Centurion (hidden for Librarian/Esoterist/Primus Nullificator Consuls) with a required Biomancy/Telepathy choice. The single power is chosen at the table.

8. **Hex-Bolts - which units.** L128: "Any Word Bearers Infantry unit equipped with Bolt weapons".
   *Built:* +5 per unit on Tactical, Assault, Breacher, Reconnaissance, Veteran, Command, Honour Guard, Terminator, Terminator Command, Destroyer, Heavy Support, Gal Vorbak, Procurator, Possessed and Covenant Zealot Mob (only the Overseer has a bolt pistol). Hidden if the unit has Special Issue Ammunition (so the Seeker Squad never gets it). Not on Praetor/Centurion or bikes.
   *Question:* Should characters (Praetor/Centurion) and the Zealot Mob be able to take it?

9. **Icon of Chaos Undivided.** L134: "One model in a Legion Command Squad, Legion Veteran Squad or Legion Terminator Squad".
   *Built:* a +30 squad option (max 1). It is not tied to a particular model. Not offered on Terminator Command Squads or Honour Guard.

10. **Favour of the Pantheon - who.** L139: "One non-named Word Bearers Independent Character in the army".
    *Built:* only Praetor and Centurion (max 1 per army, one Favour per model). Strength/Attacks/unit type change in the profile; Daemonic Aura's 5+ invulnerable save and Visage are text only. Errors if Daemonic Wings is combined with a Jump Pack, Bike or Terminator Armour.
    *Question:* Should other ICs (e.g. Techmarines) be able to take one?

11. **Diabolist.** L150-154. *Built:* Consul +35 with Daemon and Preferred Enemy (Loyalists); Bike, all Terminator Armour, Power Fist and Thunder Hammer hidden while chosen (Jetbike needs a Bike, so it is blocked too). Accursed Crozius stays available.
    *Question:* Can a Diabolist be a Loyalist (he gains Daemon)? Nothing in the text limits it to Traitor armies.

12. **Dark Channelling.** L158. *Built:* +25 on Tactical, Veteran, Breacher, Terminator and Assault Squads, only shown while the Detachment has a Diabolist, Erebus, Kor Phaeron or Zardu Layak. The roll (and Zardu's +1, L1453) is text only.

## Rites of War

13. **The Dark Brethren.** L192-196. *Enforced:* Traitor only, needs a Diabolist (or Erebus/Kor/Zardu), max one Heavy Support, no Fortification. *Text only:* no Allied Detachment from another Space Marine Legion, other allies are Desperate Allies, all Effects.

14. **Last of the Serrated Sun - Gal Vorbak cannot fit a Drop Pod.** L271: "Every Gal Vorbak unit in the Detachment must purchase either a Legion Drop Pod or Dreadclaw Drop Pod". Gal Vorbak are Bulky (L600) and can be up to 10 models (L605); both pods carry 10 models, so only 5-model squads fit.
    *Built:* under the Rite Gal Vorbak are Troops (and can fill compulsory Troops) and show an error without a Drop Pod/Dreadclaw; pods are hidden for squads of 6+.
    *Question:* Should the pod requirement ignore Bulky, or are Gal Vorbak limited to 5 models under this Rite? Does it also apply to Gal Vorbak taken as Argel Tal's or Lorgar's retinue (not enforced)?

15. **Drop Elite.** L275. *Built:* every Dedicated Transport list that has a Rhino gets "Legion Drop Pod (Drop Elite)" while the Rite is chosen.

16. **Serrated Sun limitations.** L284-287. *Enforced:* Traitor only, no Fortification. *Text only:* all Infantry in Reserve via Drop Pod/Teleport/Flyer, no Immobile units, no Allied Detachment.
    *Question:* "No Immobile units" - does that include the army's own Drop Pods (they are Immobile once landed)? Presumably not.

## Units

17. **Covenant Zealot Mob.** L430 says "EXPANDABILE" - built as Expendable. L432-444 lists no Legiones Astartes (Word Bearers) rule.
    *Built:* Troops, can fill compulsory Troops, no Legion rule (so no Fanatical Devotion).
    *Question:* Is the missing Legion rule intended? May Zealot Mobs fill compulsory Troops (built: yes)? Is "Word Bearers only" (L434) meant as Traitor only?

18. **Gal Vorbak Dedicated Transport.** L637: "may select a Land Raider, Dreadclaw Drop Pod or Spartan Assault Tank".
    *Built:* Land Raider Phobos, Land Raider Proteus, Dreadclaw, Spartan; Land Raiders and Dreadclaw hidden for 6+ models (Bulky). Orbital Assault Drop Pod option as usual.
    *Question:* Which Land Raider variants are allowed (Achilles?)?

19. **Ashen Circle "Reign of Fire".** L1457: "If Zardu Layak is the army's Warlord, Ashen Circle Squads may be selected as Troops".
    *Built:* a "Selected as Troops (Reign of Fire)" toggle on the Ashen Circle, shown only when Zardu Layak is in the army. New Recruit cannot check who the Warlord is.

20. **Possessed Champion.** L1126: "One Possessed Marine may be upgraded to a Possessed Champion for +10 points." *Built:* the Champion replaces one Possessed Marine (26+10 = 36 pts).

21. **Mhara Gal.** L867-875. *Built:* second Dreadnought CCW is a free option instead of the Plasma Cannon; Attacks become 4 when chosen; each built-in Twin-linked Bolter can be swapped separately.

## Characters

    *Question:* Does he need a Jump Pack (Daemonic wings)? Can his Gal Vorbak retinue (Infantry) keep up - should they be Jump Infantry while he leads them?

23. **Kor Phaeron's Jealous Command** (L1381) and **Hol Beloth's Hexaglyphic Ward / Exhortation** (L1586-1590): text only. New Recruit cannot check the Warlord.
    *Question:* Kor Phaeron has no Krak/Melta option and no Command Squad but a Terminator Command Squad (L1385). Correct?

24. **Zardu Layak.** Malefic Daemonology powers (L1444) are chosen at the table. Anakatis Kul (L1461, L1504) built as a +100 upgrade with the 2-model profile. Zardu has no Command Retinue - correct?

25. **Hol Beloth's Iron Halo** (L1609). *Built:* a named-character Iron Halo that does not count against any army limit of one Iron Halo.

26. **Named characters are all Master of the Legion** (Argel Tal, Erebus, Kor Phaeron, Zardu Layak, Hol Beloth) and all Traitor only. Fine as written - only one per army unless the Master of the Legion rules say otherwise.
