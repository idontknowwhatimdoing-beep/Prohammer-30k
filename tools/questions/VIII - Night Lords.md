# VIII - Night Lords - questions for the author

Source: `/home/claude/src/legions/VIII_Night_Lords.txt` (line numbers in brackets). Module: `tools/legions/viii_night_lords.py`.

1. **Terror Assault (Legion rule) vs. Rite** [12-14, 95-99] - "A Night Lords army may exchange two Heavy Support selections for one additional Fast Attack selection ... 4 Fast Attack / 1 Heavy Support." The Rite's *Rapid Strike Force* gives exactly the same chart.
   *Did:* an optional toggle on the Legion entry (FA +1, HS max 1). With the Rite chosen the same change applies automatically and the toggle is hidden.
   *Question:* Is the Legion rule really meant to be identical to the Rite's effect (making Rapid Strike Force redundant)?

2. **Night Lords Armoury and the Armoury points caps** [35-36, 48-49] - Chainglaive and Trophies are added to the Praetor/Centurion "Additional Wargear" and to every Sergeant's "Space Marine Armoury (max 50 pts)".
   *Question:* Do these items count towards the 100/50-point Armoury limits (the builder currently counts them)?

3. **Trophies of Judgement for Sergeants without Armoury access** [49] - "Any Night Lords Independent Character or squad Sergeant may purchase Trophies of Judgement".
   *Did:* offered through the Armoury (Sergeants with Armoury access) and explicitly to the Contekar Dissident. Not offered to the Raptor Sergeant unless he is a Headtaker (the squad already carries Trophies). Named characters cannot buy it.
   *Question:* Should every squad leader (even without Armoury access) be able to buy it? Does the Contekar Dissident count as a "Sergeant"?

4. **Stealth Adept** [55] - *Did:* +5 for Praetor, Centurion and Techmarines (not with Terminator Armour or a Bike); +1/model for Tactical, Assault, Breacher, Recon, Veteran, Destroyer, Seeker, Heavy Support, Command and Honour Guard Squads (not a Command Squad on Bikes), Terror and Night Raptor Squads. Named characters get no option.
   *Not enforced:* "An Independent Character joining a unit with Stealth Adept must also possess Stealth Adept" (text only).
   *Question:* May named Night Lords characters buy Stealth Adept?

5. **Kraken Light Bolts for Terror Squads** [59] - "Terror Squads may also purchase Kraken Light Bolts where stated in their army list entry." The Terror Squad entry [219-243] does not state it.
   *Did:* not offered to Terror Squads (only Tactical and Veteran Squads, +10/unit).
   *Question:* Should Terror Squads get Kraken Light Bolts (+10/unit)?

6. **Teleportation Transponders** [65-66] - *Did:* +15/unit for Legion Terminator Squads, Legion Terminator Command Squads and Contekar; +10 for a Praetor/Centurion in Terminator Armour. Not offered to the Atramentar (they already have Teleport Assault). *Question:* OK?

7. **Terror Formations wording** [88] - "Terror Squads may also be selected as Troops choices once their unit entry is included in the Night Lords army list." *Did:* treated as a normal Troops permission (Terror Squads and Night Raptors become Troops and count for the compulsory Troops). *Question:* Is the "once their unit entry is included" clause left over from a draft?

8. **Rules shown only as text (not enforced)** - Cover of Darkness, Terror Attack [81-92]; "No Fortification" [105, 145] (no Fortifications exist in this catalogue); Vox-Scream Broadcast, The Scent of Blood, no voluntary withdrawal [131-147]; Terror Made Manifest, Masters of the Terror Assault [6-10]. Enforced: at least one Night Raptor/Terror Squad (Terror Assault) or Night Raptor Squad (Horror Cult) in the Detachment, Traitor-only Horror Cult, Heavy Support 0-1.

9. **Beyond Judgement for units that already have Trophies** [125-127] - *Did:* the +25/unit option is shown (Horror Cult only) on all Infantry/Jump Infantry squads, including Terminator units (they are Infantry) and the Terror/Night Raptor/Atramentar units, which already carry Trophies - buying it gives them Fear.
   *Question:* Should squads that already have Trophies get Fear for free / be able to buy it? Are Terminator units included?

10. **Terror Squad Headsman Armoury** [224-248] - *Did:* the Headsman has the listed Chainsword and Bolter replacements; his 50-pt Armoury may replace his Bolt Pistol and buy wargear. *Question:* May the Armoury also replace his Chainsword/Bolter (e.g. with a Power Fist)?

11. **Special weapons "for every five models"** [233-237] - one per five models including the Headsman (5 models = 1, 10 = 2), Terror Marines only. OK?

12. **"Land Raider" as a Dedicated Transport** [252, 495, 637] - *Did:* Land Raider Phobos or Proteus. *Question:* Correct, or only one of them?

13. **Night Raptors "Up to five models may replace their Chainsword"** [356] - *Did:* a squad-level pool of 5 that includes the Sergeant. The Headtaker's Armoury [378] may only replace his Bolt Pistol and buy wargear; the Armoury is locked unless the Sergeant is upgraded to Headtaker. *Question:* Should a non-Headtaker Raptor Sergeant have Armoury access? May the Headtaker's Armoury replace his Chainsword?

14. **Nostraman Chainblade / Escaton Power Claw profiles** [448, 452] - *Did:* Chainblade = S User+1, Rending, Two-Handed (not a Power Weapon); Escaton Power Claw = Power Fist profile (x2, Unwieldy) with re-roll failed To Wound. *Question:* Confirm the Chainblade is not a Power Weapon (AP)?

15. **Contekar "Up to two models may replace their Nostraman Chainblade"** [486] - squad-level pool of 2 (may include the Dissident). OK?

16. **Sevatar's Terminator Command Squad** [759] - Sevatar wears Artificer Armour, but the normal rules only allow a Terminator Command Squad for a character in Terminator Armour. *Did:* allowed as written. *Question:* Intended?

17. **Iron Halo on named characters** [769] - The Space Marine Armoury says "An army may normally contain no more than one Iron Halo" and the builder enforces it for the shared Iron Halo. *Did:* Sevatar's Iron Halo is a separate item that does not count against that limit (otherwise Sevatar + a Praetor would be illegal). *Question:* Correct?

18. **Kheron Ophion's "Power Axe"** [840] - not defined anywhere. *Did:* Power Weapon profile (S User, ignores Armour Saves). *Question:* What is the Power Axe's profile?

19. **Malcharion is not Master of the Legion** [919-922] - his special rules do not list Master of the Legion (Sevatar, Ophion and Shang have it). *Did:* not Master of the Legion; may still fulfil the compulsory HQ. *Question:* Intended? Also: his Legion Veteran Squad retinue uses the normal Veteran Squad entry (it never counts as Troops). OK?

20. **Mawdrym Llansahai** [1042] - stray "#" after "Warlord."; "may never be the army's Warlord" is text only. Not Master of the Legion, not eligible for the compulsory HQ. OK?

21. **Konrad Curze's save** [1181, 1285] - profile prints "1+"; the Nightmare Mantle counts as Primarch Armour (1+ / 4+ invulnerable). *Did:* profile shows "1+/4+". OK?

22. **"Bloody Murder"** [1206] - listed in Curze's special rules but never defined. *Did:* shown as a placeholder rule. *Question:* What does Bloody Murder do?

23. **Night Raptor Retinue** [1327] - "the unit may use his Hit & Run special rule" - Night Raptor Squads already have Hit & Run [348]. *Question:* Was a different benefit intended?

24. **Curze's Primarch Retinue** [1319-1323] - the list does not include a Legion Honour Guard Squad (the universal Primarch Retinue rule does). *Did:* only the four listed units. *Question:* Should Honour Guard also be allowed?

25. **Allegiance** - Curze, Sevatar, Shang and Malcharion have no Allegiance restriction; Ophion and Mawdrym are Traitor only (enforced). OK?
