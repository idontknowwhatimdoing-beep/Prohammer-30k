# XVI - Sons of Horus: questions for the author

Source: `/home/claude/src/legions/XVI_Sons_of_Horus.txt` (line numbers below). Module: `tools/legions/xvi_sons_of_horus.py`.

## Armoury

1. **Banestrike Ammunition - 5 or 2 points per model?** L36-37 "BANESTRIKE AMMUNITION — 5 POINTS PER MODEL ... Justaerin
   Terminator Squads ... +5 points per eligible model", but the Justaerin entry L249 says "+2 points per eligible model".
   Done: Justaerin +2 per eligible model (entry price), Seeker Squads and Independent Characters +5. Question: which is
   right for Justaerin?
2. **Banestrike - which Independent Characters?** (L37) Done: Praetor and Centurion (all Consuls) may buy it (+5, counts
   towards the Armoury cap, hidden unless they carry a Bolter / Foeblaster / Combi-bolter / Storm Bolter / Combi-weapon).
   Aximand (carries a Bolter) was also given it as an option (+5) although his entry only lists Krak grenades (L823-824);
   Abaddon has it as fixed wargear (L740). Question: may named characters buy it (Aximand)?
3. **Banestrike on Seeker Squads replaces Special Issue Ammunition?** L53 "may not be combined with Special Issue
   Ammunition". Done: option "Banestrike Ammunition (all eligible models, replaces Special Issue Ammunition)" +5 per model
   that kept a bolt weapon (special weapons don't pay). The Seekers' Special Issue Ammunition rule is still shown.
   Question: does the squad simply choose one ammunition per shot/battle, or lose Special Issue Ammunition entirely?
4. **Cthonian Culling Blade - extra item or replacement?** (L57 "may purchase ... for +10 points".) Done: extra Armoury
   item (+10, counts towards the 100/50-pt caps) for Praetor, Centurion and every Sergeant/Champion 50-pt Armoury (this
   includes Terminator Sergeants and the Reaver Chieftain / Chieftain Prime). Question: should it replace a weapon, and
   should Terminator-armoured Sergeants get it?
5. **Chainaxe for Characters** (L71). Done: every Character choice that offers a Chainsword or a Close Combat Weapon also
   offers a Chainaxe (+4). Fixed wargear (e.g. Maloghurst's Close-combat weapon, L962) was not changed. OK?
6. **Teleportation Transponders for named characters?** (L86) Done: Terminator Squads, Terminator Command Squads (also as
   retinues) and Justaerin +15 per unit; Praetor/Centurion in Terminator armour +10. Abaddon (Cthonian Dreadplate counts
   as Cataphractii, L715) was not given the option because his entry lists no options. Question: may Abaddon buy them?

## Rites of War

7. **The Long March - Terminators as non-compulsory Troops** (L107-108). Done: with the Rite, Legion Terminator Squads
   become Troops but not "Compulsory Troops Eligible". Only the "Traitor only" limitation (L112) is enforced (error).
   Not enforced (text only): Slow and Purposeful restriction (L113), no Fortification / Allied Detachment (L114),
   Relentless March, The Warmaster's Portion.
8. **The Black Reaving** (L124-149). Done: Reaver Attack Squads become Troops and count as compulsory Troops; errors if
   the Detachment has no Master of Signals Consul or no Reaver Attack Squad. The builder cannot check that the Reaver is
   one of the *compulsory* choices, nor "at least as many Fast Attack as Heavy Support choices" (L148, New Recruit cannot
   compare two counts), nor "no Fortification or Allied Detachment" (L149). Text only: Cthonian Encirclement, Lodge
   Ceremony, Cut Them Apart. Do Reavers taken as Troops still count as Fast Attack for the L148 check?

## Units

9. **Justaerin 0-1 and retinues.** L198-201: a Praetor, Abaddon or Horus may take a Justaerin Squad as retinue, "0-1".
   Done: the Elites Justaerin Squad is 0-1 per Detachment (+1 with Abaddon in the Detachment, +1 if one is taken as Troops
   via The First Company). Retinue squads (Praetor, Abaddon, Horus) are separate entries and do NOT count towards the
   0-1. The book only says so for Horus (L1409). Question: does a Praetor's / Abaddon's Justaerin retinue count against
   the 0-1? Also "if Abaddon is the army's Warlord" (L719) is not checked - having Abaddon in the Detachment is enough.
10. **Justaerin weapons.** L215 "Power weapon or Power fist", L227 "Any model equipped with a Power fist may replace it
    with Chainfist +5". Done: each model has a Power Weapon by default and may swap it for a Power Fist (free) or
    Chainfist (+5). OK?
11. **Falkus Kibre's Furious Charge** (L1185). Done: the "Furious Charge Veteran Skill (+4/model)" option appears in a
    normal Justaerin Squad only while Falkus is in it; always in Abaddon's retinue (L720); free in Horus' retinue
    (L1410). Fearless from Justaerin Primus / Master of the Justaerin is text only.
12. **Reaver Attack Squad - overlapping weapon options.** L361-371: "up to two Reavers replace Bolt pistol" and "one per
    five may replace Bolt pistol and Chainaxe with Lightning Claws". Done as two independent pools; the builder does not
    stop one Reaver from using both. "Land Raider" (L396, L512, L258) was built as Land Raider Phobos or Proteus for
    Reavers, Chieftains and Justaerin. OK?
13. **Chieftain Squad as retinue** (L457 "Praetor or appropriate named Sons of Horus Independent Character"). Done: the
    Praetor and Aximand (his Command Retinue, L792) may take one. Ashurhaddon's Master of the True Sons mentions Chieftain
    Squads (L1101) but his retinue (L1097) is Reavers only. Which named characters are "appropriate"?
14. **Luperci Pack** (L551-632). Built as listed; Damned (no Scoring, only Daemon ICs may join) is text only.

## Horus

20. **Horus' Primarch Retinue** (L1404-1411). Done: Honour Guard, Terminator Command Squad or Justaerin (Furious Charge
    free, Falkus allowed, does not count against the 0-1). Same list for Horus Ascended (L1582).

    Justaerin plus two Terminator Squads are switched.

    army (roster-wide, also in other Detachments). Lance Strike shown as a once-per-battle weapon. OK?

    Servo-automata), vehicles, Dreadnoughts, Primarchs. Characters inside squads pay the per-model price with their squad.
    Should Techmarines be able to take a Blessing, and at which price?
