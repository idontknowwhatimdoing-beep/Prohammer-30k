## Shown as text only (not enforced)

- Deliverers taking a Pair of Raven's Talons (l.509-510, "replaces Combi Bolter too"): the Talons use up that model's
  Combi-bolter swap, but the standard Combi-bolter of the Deliverer models can't be removed per model in the builder
  (the Chieftain's is removed).
- Independent Characters' Sniper Rifle (l.19, "instead of a bolter, otherwise additionally"): Praetors/Centurions have
  no Bolter as standard, so it is offered as an additional 2-pt item that does not count towards the Armoury cap; a
  Bolter bought from the Armoury is not removed automatically.
