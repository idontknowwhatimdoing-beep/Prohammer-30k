## Ambiguities / decisions

1. **Fulcrum Hand Cannon: which characters?** (l.49: "Any Raven Guard Independent Character or squad Sergeant with
   access to the Space Marine Armoury may replace one or two Bolt Pistols ...")
   Your answer "Only true Sergeants, or any unit-upgrade character with Armoury access otherwise" could be read either
   way. Built: every unit-upgrade character with Armoury access and a Bolt Pistol gets it (Sergeants, Command Squad
   Champion/Apothecary/Standard Bearer, Honour Guard Champion, Dark Fury Strike Leader, Mor Deythan Sergeant, Raptor
   Alpha). Q: Should Champions/Apothecaries/Standard Bearers/Strike Leader/Raptor Alpha lose the option (true Sergeants
   only)?

2. **Destroyer Sergeants "Must replace both"** (l.49). Built as an optional "Two Fulcrum Hand Cannons (replaces Two
   Bolt Pistols)" for +20 (he can't replace just one), not as a compulsory swap. Q: Correct, or must every Destroyer
   Sergeant carry two Fulcrum Hand Cannons?

3. **Raven's Talons upgrade for Lightning Claws** (l.42-43: "A Raven Guard Character already equipped with a pair of
   Lightning Claws may upgrade them to Raven's Talons for +5 points.") Per your answer, characters with Lightning Claws
   get no access to Raven's Talons, so the builder no longer offers this upgrade. The sentence is still in the book.
   Q: Remove it from the book?

4. **Kaedes Nex "Dual Pistols"** (l.736). The Legiones Astartes "Dual Pistols" rule text is written for the Moritat
   ("A Moritat may fire both of his pistols ..."), so Nex links the generic "Dual Pistols (Destroyers)" rule ("A model
   with this special rule may fire both of its Pistol weapons ..."). Q: OK?

## Shown as text only (not enforced)

- Deliverers taking a Pair of Raven's Talons (l.510-511, "replaces Combi Bolter too"): the Talons use up that model's
  Combi-bolter swap, but the standard Combi-bolter of the Deliverer models can't be removed per model in the builder
  (the Chieftain's is removed).
- Independent Characters' Sniper Rifle (l.19, "instead of a bolter, otherwise additionally"): Praetors/Centurions have
  no Bolter as standard, so it is offered as an additional 2-pt item that does not count towards the Armoury cap; a
  Bolter bought from the Armoury is not removed automatically.
