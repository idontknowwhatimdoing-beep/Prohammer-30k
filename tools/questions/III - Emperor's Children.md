## Unclear / missing points

1. **Phoenix Spear cost (L122)**: "may instead select a Phoenix Spear for +20 points. A model already equipped with a Power Weapon as part of its basic wargear may exchange it for ... +5 points."
   Built: next to every Power Weapon option of a Character the spear costs 20 points in total (e.g. Praetor Armoury: Power Weapon 15 / Phoenix Spear 20; Sergeant "replace Chainsword": Power Weapon +10 / Phoenix Spear +20). Where the Power Weapon is the model's free basic wargear (Honour Guard Champion, Command Squad Champion, Terminator characters) the spear costs +5. Named characters cannot take it.
   Question: is +20 the full price of the spear (as built) or +20 on top of the Power Weapon's cost? May named characters (e.g. Lucius, L1095) exchange their Power Weapon?

2. **Digital Lasers and Sonic Shrieker for Independent Characters (L133, L138)**: built as options in the Praetor/Centurion "Additional Wargear" (they count towards the 100-point Armoury cap). Named characters cannot take them.
   Question: should they count towards the Armoury cap, and may named Independent Characters (Eidolon, Tarvitz, Lucius, Bile) purchase them?

3. **Sonic Weaponry for a Heavy Support Squad (L147)**: "may select up to four Sonic Weapons instead of selecting weapons from its normal Heavy Weapon options."
   Built: a "Sonic Weaponry (Traitor only)" toggle on the Legion Heavy Support Squad (max one per Detachment, two with 3rd Company Elite). While it is chosen the squad may take up to four Sonic Weapons and none of its normal Heavy Weapons.
   Question: may a squad mix Sonic Weapons and normal Heavy Weapons (up to four in total)? Do the Sonic Weapons replace the Bolter of the model that carries them?

4. **Sonic Weapon for a Praetor or Centurion (L152-155)**: built as a separate option (one per army, Traitor only) that does not count towards the Armoury points cap. Question: should it count towards the 100-point Armoury cap? May a Legion Consul (e.g. a Librarian) take it?

5. **Perfect Cacophony, Armoury (L182)**: "A Legion Heavy Support Squad in which the majority of surviving models are equipped with Sonic Weapons may purchase Fearless for +20". A Heavy Support Squad has 5-10 models but may take at most four Sonic Weapons, so a majority is only possible with 5-7 models (and 8+ models never qualify).
   Built: the +20 Fearless option appears when the squad has chosen Sonic Weaponry; the majority is not checked. Question: is that the intent? Also the 3rd Company Elite Rite has a different rule with the same name (L286-287) - should one be renamed?

6. **Sonic Weapons are Traitor only (L145, L158), but Lord Commander Eidolon (L953) and Phoenix Terminators (L507-508) carry/may take a Doom Siren.** Built: Eidolon and the Phoenix Terminator Doom Siren are not restricted to Traitors. Question: are Eidolon / the Phoenix Doom Siren Traitor only?

7. **Sonic Blaster profile (L164-168)**: "Assault 2 or Heavy 3". Built as two profiles (Sonic Blaster - Assault / - Heavy) with the same range, S and AP. Question: is that right (no other difference between modes)?

8. **Purity Above All (L13-15)**: built: Legion Veteran Sergeants may be upgraded (any number, +25); one Legion Tactical or Assault Sergeant per army (+25, enforced over the whole roster). The upgraded model gets a Narthecium and may buy a Reductor (+5). Question: "in the army" - per army (as built) or per Detachment?

9. **3rd Company Elite, Sonic Assault (L277)**: "Any Emperor's Children Infantry unit composed entirely of models wearing Power Armour or Artificer Armour may purchase Sonic Shriekers for +2 points per model."
   Built for: Legion Tactical, Assault, Veteran, Destroyer, Seeker, Heavy Support, Command and Honour Guard Squads, Palatine Blade, Kakophoni and Sun Killer Squads (whole unit, +2 per model, only under this Rite). Not for Breachers (Hardened Armour), Recon (Recon Armour), Terminators, Techmarine Covenants or Bikes/Jetbikes.
   Question: do Jump Infantry units (Assault Squads, Palatine Blades with Jump Packs) count as "Infantry"? Should Bikes/Jetbikes (Power Armour) be included?

10. **3rd Company Elite Warlord (L293-295)**: "be a named Character whose rules specifically associate him with the Kakophoni or 3rd Company". None of the named characters in this book has such a rule. Question: does Eidolon (who carries a Doom Siren) qualify? Not enforced (text only).

11. **3rd Company Elite limits (L292, L296)**: "The army must include at least one Kakophoni Squad" and "no more than one Fortification" are checked per Detachment. The Fortification limit is already the normal FOC maximum. Question: per army or per Detachment?

12. **The Maru Skara, Immobile units (L261)**: built as an error for Legion Drop Pods and Legion Dreadnought Drop Pods in the Detachment. Question: which other units count as Immobile (Fortifications? Rapier batteries?)
