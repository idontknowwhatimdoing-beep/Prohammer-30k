# I - Dark Angels: questions for the author

Source: `/home/claude/src/legions/I_Dark_Angels.txt` (line numbers below). Module: `tools/legions/i_dark_angels.py`.

## Legion rules

1. **Which units choose a Wing?** (L14 "Every Dark Angels unit must be assigned to one of the following Wings".)
   Done: every root unit and every retinue gets a required "Hexagrammaton Wing" choice. Dedicated Transports get none
   (L22: they share their unit's Wing). Units with a printed Wing have it fixed. The Knights Cenobium get none (L839).
   Lion El'Jonson gets none because his entry names no Wing. Question: should the Lion (and other Primarch-type models)
   have a Wing? Should Lords of War, Fortifications or allied units choose one?
2. **Mastery of the Blade - "equal Weapon Skill"** (L7). Shown as text only; the builder cannot enforce it. The same
   applies to every Wing bonus (L27-60), to Dreadwing's Strength reduction and to Ravenwing's Skilled Rider/Jink.

## Armoury

3. **Terranic Greatsword - purchase or replacement?** (L100 "may purchase a Terranic Greatsword for +35 points".)
   Done: an extra choice (+35) in the Praetor's and Centurion's "Replace Bolt Pistol" and "Replace Chainsword" slots.
   This includes Consuls, because a Consul is a Centurion upgrade. It does not count towards the 100-pt Armoury cap.
   Because the sword is Two-Handed, an error appears if a model takes two. Question: should it instead be an additional
   Armoury item that counts towards the cap? May Sergeants or Terminator-armoured characters take it?
4. **Calibanite Warblade - who counts as a Character?** (L89.) Done: +10 on top of the Power Weapon's cost, wherever a
   Power Weapon can be chosen by a model whose unit type includes "Character". This covers Praetor, Centurion, Sergeants,
   Champions, Oathbearers and so on. Models with a fixed Power Weapon get a "Replace Power Weapon" slot (+10). Question:
   should non-character squad members (e.g. Veterans with Power Weapons) also get it?
5. **Ancient Plasma / Plasma Repeater / Plasma Burner** (L111, L135, L149). Done: wherever a Plasma Pistol/Gun/Cannon can
   be chosen, the Calibanite version is offered at the same price. Wherever a Plasma Gun can be chosen, the Repeater
   (+10) and Burner (+5) are also offered. Fixed (not selectable) plasma weapons and Twin-linked/Combi plasma weapons were
   not changed, except Redloss' pistol (see 20). Question: should vehicle/Dreadnought plasma weapons and Combi-plasma
   guns be included?
6. **Plasma Flame / Plasma Burner profile.** L160 says "Roll once for the number of shots generated by each Plasma Burner".
   L1064 says one roll applies to every identical weapon in the unit. Done: the rule text includes both (one roll per
   unit). Correct?
7. **Stasis Shells - "Where another army list entry provides access to a Grenade Launcher"** (L164). Done: +5 per
   Missile Launcher (all Missile Launcher variants, Destroyer Rad-missile launchers included) and +5 per Combi-Grenade
   Launcher. The Enigmatus launcher already includes Stasis Grenades. Question: does a Combi-Grenade Launcher count as a
   Grenade Launcher?
8. **Molecular Acid Shells - "Any Dark Angels model or Dreadnought"** (L184). Done: +7 per Heavy Bolter / Twin-linked
   Heavy Bolter (and Suspensor variants) on infantry, bikes and Dreadnoughts. Non-walker Vehicles (Land Speeders,
   Predator sponsons and so on) and Sky Hunter Scimitar Jetbikes (their Heavy Bolter is part of the jetbike item) did not
   get it. Question: should Vehicles or Sky Hunters get it?
9. **Teleportation Transponders** (L201). Done: +15 per unit for Legion Terminator Squads, Terminator Command Squads,
   Deathwing Terminator Companions and Knights Cenobium. +10 for a Praetor/Centurion in Terminator Armour (hidden
   otherwise), Marduk Sedras and Holguin. The builder does not check that "a unit composed entirely of models wearing
   Terminator Armour" applies to any other unit. OK?

## Rites of War

10. **Warlord must belong to <Wing>** (L230, L263, L323, L371, L415, L461). Text only. New Recruit has no Warlord
    selection here. Should a "Warlord" toggle be added (a shared change)?
11. **The Storm of War - Centurion inside a 20-model squad** (L211-218). Done: Tactical and Assault Squads get a hidden
    "Legion Centurion (Masters of the Storm of War)" model. It appears only with this Rite and 20 models, has full
    options, has no Consul or retinue, and is fixed to the Stormwing. "May not be the Warlord" is text only. Only
    20-model Stormwing Tactical/Assault Squads count as compulsory Troops.
12. **The Storm of War - "Compulsory Troops choices may not select Dedicated Transports"** (L233). The builder cannot tell
    which Troops choices are the compulsory ones, so this is text only. Should all 20-model Stormwing Tactical/Assault
    Squads be barred from transports instead?
13. **The Unbroken Vow / The Eskaton Imperative / The Seeker's Arrow / The Serpent's Bane / The Steel Fist** - compulsory
    Troops. Done: the named units become Troops and count as compulsory Troops only with the right Wing. Under The
    Eskaton Imperative the Interemptor Squad becomes Troops and always counts (it is always Dreadwing, L301/L324). Under
    The Steel Fist, Predator Strike Squadrons get a "Selected as Troops" toggle with no 0-2 limit (L351-352: "may be
    selected as Troops" - is there a limit?).
14. **The Steel Fist - Armoured Assault** (L356-360). Done: Ironwing Infantry units with a Dedicated Transport get an extra
    group. It offers a Land Raider Phobos/Proteus (10 models or fewer) or a Spartan (11-20 models), and an error appears
    if a unit has two Dedicated Transports. Units fixed to another Wing (Deathwing Companions, Interemptors...) and the
    Knights Cenobium do not get it. Transport Capacity is not checked. "Every Infantry unit which can be transported must
    begin embarked" (L373) and "Marshal of the Steel Fist" are text only.
15. **The Steel Fist "no more than one Fast Attack choice"** (L374) and **The Seeker's Arrow "no more than one Heavy
    Support choice"** (L417). These use the shared FA/HS limit categories. "No Vehicle unless Fast and/or Skimmer"
    (L418) is an error that lists every vehicle/walker entry without Fast or Skimmer in its type. This includes Dedicated
    Transports such as Rhinos and Drop Pods. Correct that Drop Pods are forbidden too?
16. **Text-only Rite effects**: Disciplined Volleys, Officers of the Storm, Death is not the End, Marshal of the Unbroken
    Vow, The Oath and its VP penalty, Masters of the Blackened Earth, Walkers in Ash, Marshal of the Eskaton and its VP
    penalty, Encirclement, Lightning Assault, The Seeker's Arrow bonus, The Serpent's Heads / Execution Protocols /
    Marshal of the Firewing / Forward Deployment (up to three Firewing Troops units) and the Priority Target VP penalty.

## Units

17. **Deathwing Companion Detachment - who may take it as a retinue?** (L592 "a Dark Angels Praetor or an appropriate
    named Dark Angels Character".) Done: it is in the Retinue choice of the Praetor, Corswain and the Lion. Its Jump
    Packs (+15/model) appear only for the Praetor's version and only if he has a Jump Pack. It can also be taken as a
    normal Elites choice (L529). Question: which other named characters are "appropriate" (Redloss? Holguin?)? May a
    Centurion take it?
18. **Deathwing Terminator Companions - "eligible Dark Angels Character"** (L777). Done: available as a retinue for the
    Praetor and Centurion when they wear Terminator Armour. An error appears if the squad's pattern differs from the
    character's. Holguin's version is fixed to Cataphractii. The Lion's version allows either pattern (L1960). Marduk
    Sedras did not get it (he has the Cenobium Retinue). Question: are Centurions/Consuls eligible? Should Sedras be?
    The unit name in the header is "Deathwing Terminator Companions" (L678), while the rules call it "Detachment"
    (L777, L1661). Done: named "Deathwing Terminator Companions".
19. **Knights Cenobium transport** (L909). "A unit of five models may select a Land Raider. A unit of up to ten models may
    select a Spartan." Done: Land Raider Phobos/Proteus only at exactly five models; Spartan always. The unit lists
    "Mastery of the Blade" although its Special Rules (L889-895) do not name it: it comes with the Legiones Astartes (Dark
    Angels) rule. OK?

## Named characters and Primarch

20. **Farith Redloss' Plasma Pistol** (L1614). Done: a slot that lets it be swapped for a Calibanite Plasma Pistol at no
    cost (Ancient Plasma Weaponry, L111). The rest of his kit is fixed, including 2 Phosphex Bombs. OK, or should his
    pistol be fixed?
21. **Marduk Sedras - "Combi-volkite"** (L1526). Done: Combi-Volkite Charger. He gets Siege Specialists through Eskaton
    (L1511). Its text is taken from the Alpha Legion Veteran Skills (XX_Alpha_Legion.txt L14). The rule that units he
    joins also gain it is text only. "Cenobium Retinue" and "Siege Specialists" are not in his Special Rules list
    (L1531-1538) but come from his rules text, so they are listed too.
22. **Corswain - Armour of the Forest** (L1362). The army book gives no rules for it. Done: a 2+ save and a 4+
    invulnerable save from his profile. Seneschal of the First Legion (L1347): +5/model for WS+1 (max 6), only on
    Corswain's retinue. Done: one squad-wide option that sets WS to 6 (Companions are WS5). OK?
23. **Death of Worlds** (L1496-1501). Done: Strength 6 (not User-based), Power Weapon, Two-Handed, Murderous Strike (5+).
24. **Lion El'Jonson** (L1792-1960). Done: 550 pts, Lord of War, Loyalist only, with a free choice of the Lion Sword or the
    Wolf Blade. His Primarch Retinue can be a Legion Honour Guard Squad, a Terminator Command Squad, Deathwing Companions,
    or Deathwing Terminator Companions (either pattern). The Fusil Actinaeus profile is taken from L1938-1939. The header
    at L1928 ("FUSIL ACTINAEUSSTASIS GRENADES") looks like a typo for two headings.
25. **Named characters "Master of the Legion"**. Corswain, Sedras, Redloss and Holguin all have Master of the Legion
    (L1372, L1534, L1623, L1691). Done as printed. They need no minimum points, and allegiance is not limited (none is
    printed). OK?
